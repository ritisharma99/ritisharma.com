The best way to understand terrain is to drive through it, preferably before dawn, on a road that isn't on most maps. I've done long drives through the mountains of New Zealand, India, and the US East Coast. There's a specific kind of thinking that only happens when the landscape keeps changing and you have nothing to do but pay attention.
