There's something satisfying about designing a part, sending it to a machine, and watching it come out the other end. I 3D print scale models, replacement parts, and mechanical assemblies, sometimes because it's useful, sometimes just because I want to see if I can. Failed prints teach you more than successful ones. The layer lines are a useful reminder that all structures have a hierarchy.
